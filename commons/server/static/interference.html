What Is This?

Two language models are asked the same question. Their raw cognitive traces — the "thinking" process, complete with real-time self-editing (backspaces, cursor movements, ANSI escape codes) — are captured.

Then the two streams are interleaved character by character. The result is a single "thought" that belongs to neither model — a voice that exists only in the interference pattern between them.

This is not an ensemble. Not a debate. Not a comparison. It is two cognitive streams woven at the finest grain to create a third voice that has never existed and will never exist again.

The Principle

What It Is

  • Two cognitive streams interleaved at the character level
  • A voice that belongs to neither source model
  • An interference pattern — like two waves crossing
  • A third mind that exists only for the duration of one response
  • Raw traces preserved: ANSI codes, backspaces, self-editing

What It Is Not

  • Not an ensemble (no voting, no averaging)
  • Not a debate (no turn-taking, no rebuttal)
  • Not a comparison (no side-by-side analysis)
  • Not a fusion (no semantic merging, no summarization)
  • Not a persona (no consistent identity across runs)
